Our Story · Meet the Owner

Hi, I’m Melody, the founder of Meli’s Prive. 🤍

Meli’s Prive is a reflection of my love for beautiful things, creativity, elegance and the desire to build something of my own.

I started this brand with a small beginning and a big vision. Along the way, I’ve learned that building a business is not always easy, but every order, every customer and every new step reminds me why I started.

For me, Meli’s Prive is not simply about selling products. It’s about creating an experience where you can discover something beautiful, useful or special and feel confident choosing it for yourself or someone you love.

I’m still growing, learning and building—and I’m so grateful to have you here as part of the journey.
